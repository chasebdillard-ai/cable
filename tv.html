Amazon Fire TV App · Downloader code 4793854

  1. On the Fire TV home screen, search for Downloader (orange icon, by AFTVnews) and install it.
  2. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Install unknown apps and turn Downloader ON. (No Developer Options? Settings → My Fire TV → About → click your device name 7 times.)
  3. Open Downloader, type 4793854 in the box and press Go.
  4. Press Install, then Open. Cable shows up in Your Apps & Channels — move it to the front row if you like.

Cable powers on to your last channel with sound. The screen stays awake while you watch. Use the remote as listed under Remote buttons: Menu (☰) opens the Guide, ▶▶ / ◀◀ change channels.

Direct link (if you'd rather not use the code): https://chasebdillard-ai.github.io/tv-apps/cable.apk

Google TV / Android TV Same app · code 4793854

Works on Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, Sony / TCL / Hisense / Philips Google TV and Android TV sets, and NVIDIA Shield.

  1. Install Downloader by AFTVnews from the Google Play Store on the TV.
  2. Turn on developer mode: Settings → System → About, click Android TV OS build 7 times.
  3. Allow installs: Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → Downloader ON.
  4. Open Downloader, enter 4793854, press Go, then Install.

Cable appears in the Apps row with its own TV banner. On the Google TV remote: OK = Info, ▲▼ = channels, Back closes menus (press twice on live TV to leave the app).

Android phones & tablets App or Home-screen shortcut

Option A — the app (same file as the TV app; it turns itself into the phone layout):

  1. On the phone, open Download Cable for Android and tap the download when it finishes.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

Option B — no install: open chasebdillard-ai.github.io/cable in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen). Cable opens full screen with its own icon, like an app. Samsung Internet: ☰ → Add page to → Home screen.

Works on foldables and dual-screen phones too (Surface Duo, Galaxy Fold): portrait puts the TV on top with the guide below; rotate for full-width TV.

iPhone & iPad Add to Home Screen

  1. Open chasebdillard-ai.github.io/cable in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (⇧) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open Cable from the Home Screen: it runs full screen with no Safari bars and the Cable icon. Tap Press OK to watch TV once to turn the sound on (Apple requires one tap).

There is no App Store version. To get Cable onto the big screen from an iPhone or iPad, see AirPlay.

Roku Live channels play on Roku

Roku can't run web pages, so the Roku version is a separate Roku channel:

  • Plays right on the Roku: the live networks 0.1 Right Now TV, 0.2 COMET, 0.3 Charge!, 0.4 Fubo Sports Network, 0.5 Bally Sports Live, 0.6 ION, 0.7 ION Plus and 0.8 Yahoo Sports Network (▲▼ flips between them).
  • Shows what's On Now / Up Next for every other channel, on the same schedule as the website.
  • Can't play inside the Roku channel: channels and ON DEMAND programs that come from YouTube. Choosing one opens it in Roku's own YouTube channel, from the beginning (not in sync with the schedule). That needs the YouTube channel installed and Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps → Network access set to Default or Permissive.

Install (developer side-load, for your own Roku):

  1. On the Roku remote press Home ×3, Up ×2, Right, Left, Right, Left, Right. Turn on the developer installer and set a password; note the Roku's IP address shown.
  2. On a computer on the same Wi-Fi, download cable-roku.zip.
  3. Open http://<Roku IP address> in a browser, sign in as rokudev with your password, choose Upload → cable-roku.zip → Install with zip.

Easier for everything else on Roku: cast or mirror the website from a phone or computer (Roku supports AirPlay on most 4K models and screen mirroring from Android/Windows).

Samsung, LG and other smart TV browsers No install

  1. Open the TV's Internet / Web Browser app (Samsung: Internet; LG: Web Browser).
  2. Type aftv.news/9792225 (forwards to Cable in TV mode), or chasebdillard-ai.github.io/cable/?tv=1.
  3. Press OK on Press OK to watch TV. Bookmark the page for next time.

The TV remote works: arrows, OK, CH +/−, number keys, Back, and the color buttons ■ Record, ■ Captions, ■ My DVR, ■ ON DEMAND.

Built-in TV browsers are slower than the apps and some older models can't play the YouTube-based channels; on those, use a Fire TV / Google TV stick or casting.

Apple TV, AirPlay & Chromecast Put a phone or computer on the TV

  • Apple TV has no web browser and Cable isn't in the App Store, so there's no Apple TV app. From an iPhone or iPad: open Control Center → Screen Mirroring → your Apple TV (or an AirPlay 2 TV — Samsung, LG, Vizio, Roku). From a Mac: Control Center → Screen Mirroring.
  • Chromecast with Google TV / Google TV Streamer: install the app (see Google TV) — best option.
  • Older Chromecast (no remote): in Chrome on a computer, ⋮ → Cast… → Cast tab; from Android, Quick Settings → Cast / Screen cast.
  • Xbox: the Microsoft Edge app opens the website like a smart TV browser.

Mirroring sends the whole phone screen, so keep the phone awake and on the Cable page.
